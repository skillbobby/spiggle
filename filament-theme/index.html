FAQ

Quick answers

What does this plugin do?

Spiggle Theme restyles your Laravel Filament admin panel: sidebar layouts, color palettes, dark mode, brand, fonts, and icons. You change those from Theme Settings in the panel — no custom CSS. It does not add resources or replace your forms and tables.

What do I get after purchase?

A licensed Composer package for Laravel Filament. Install it, and Theme Settings appears in the panel. No license-key screen — the paid zip is the license.

How many sites can I use it on?

Unlimited. The $9.99 license is a single lifetime purchase for all of your Laravel Filament apps.

Which versions are supported?

Laravel 13 and Laravel Filament 5 are the primary target.

Can I translate Theme Settings?

Yes. English ships in the package. Publish filament-spiggle-theme-translations and add another locale folder.

Does this replace my Laravel Filament resources?

No. It restyles the panel shell — sidebar, canvas, cards, header, icons. Your resources, forms, and tables stay yours.
