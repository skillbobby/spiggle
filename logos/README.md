# Product logos and banners

Product marks used by the landing page and the generated product banners.

- `tickitdex-dark.svg` — TickitDex wordmark for dark backgrounds, from
  https://tickitdex.com/images/tickitdex-dark.svg
- `tickitdex.svg` — TickitDex wordmark for light backgrounds, from
  https://tickitdex.com/images/tickitdex.svg
- `filawarden.svg` — FilaWarden shield mark, from
  https://filawarden.com/filawarden_logo.svg

The 1280 × 720 product banners live in `../cards/` and embed these marks:
`tickitdex-banner.svg` and `filawarden-banner.svg`.
