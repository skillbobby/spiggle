Features

A settled shell that keeps Filament

Tables, forms, and resources stay yours. The theme restyles the frame — sidebar, top bar, cards, and the sign-in screen — from the Appearance page.

One rounded frame

Sidebar, top bar, and content share a single rounded surface, including the sign-in screen.

Six colour schemes

Electric Blue, Emerald, Violet, Amber, Rose, and Teal. The active scheme paints navigation, buttons, and cards.

Appearance page

People switch schemes from the top bar or the Appearance page. The choice is stored on the user, or in the session.

Dashboard cards

A twelve-column dashboard and StatCard for each metric. The figures stay in your application.

Comfortable or compact

Keep the roomy default, or tighten type and gaps when the panel is mostly tables.

Plus Jakarta Sans

Headings and the interface use Plus Jakarta Sans. Inter is the fallback, and you can set another family.

Your brand stays yours

Set the brand name and logo on the panel. The identity block sits at the foot of the sidebar.

No theme build

The stylesheet ships with the package. Register the plugin and run php artisan filament:assets.

FAQ

Quick answers

What does this theme do?

It restyles a Filament panel: a rounded frame, six colour schemes, an appearance switcher, and the cards for a dashboard. Your resources, models, and copy stay in your application.

How many sites can I use it on?

Unlimited. The $19 license is a single lifetime purchase for all of your Laravel Filament apps.

Do I need a Vite or Tailwind rebuild?

No. The theme CSS ships with the package. Register the plugin, run php artisan filament:assets, and the panel picks it up.

Which versions are supported?

Laravel 13 and Filament 5. PHP 8.3 or newer.

Is this the same as Spiggle Theme?

No. Spiggle Theme is the layout-and-palette kit. Small Business Theme is the rounded frame, the six schemes, and the dashboard cards.

How do I get the package?

Buy the $19 lifetime license. After purchase you receive the Composer package.
