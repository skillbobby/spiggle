Who we are

A small studio behind Laravel & Filament products

We design and maintain the themes, packages and starter kits we use on client panels every day. Nothing is a demo: every release is installed, tested and documented.

Spiggle is an independent product studio. The stack is Laravel, Filament, Livewire and Tailwind CSS.

What we do

  1. 01

    Themes

    Complete admin shells: navigation, palettes, light and dark, and a settings page your team can actually use.

  2. 02

    Plugins

    Focused packages that add one job to a panel and stay out of the way. Tables, boards, billing, snapshots, fields.

  3. 03

    Starter kits

    Ready operations screens you can adopt as a reference or a starting point for a real product.

Themes, panel packages and starter kits for Filament

Themes, panel packages and starter kits for Filament 4 and 5. Every card links to the product page or the repository.

11 packages

  • small-business-theme

    Rounded frame, six colour schemes and dashboard cards for small teams. One payment, unlimited sites.

  • fascia

    Compact icon rail, flyout or large sidebar. Seven skins, Nunito, light and dark, and a mobile drawer.

  • soffit

    Soft admin shell with a full-height white sidebar, rounded cards, quiet shadows, teal and brass accents.

  • spiggle-theme

    Crisp Filament theme with standard, solid and canvas sidebar layouts plus several palettes.

  • material-theme

    Material Design chrome for Filament 5, with list view, notifications, account screens and theme settings.

  • portal-snapshot

    Create, schedule, export and restore database snapshots, including a midnight demo reset.

  • filawarden-core

    Health scoring, deployment pre-flight, queue and cron oversight, security center and telemetry.

  • rules-engine

    UI-driven event triggers for Laravel and Filament. Conditions and actions without a code deploy.

  • form-builder-core

    Public forms, a submissions inbox and CSV export. Community edition, MIT licensed.

  • dynamic-fields-core

    Custom EAV fields for any Eloquent model, surfaced in Filament forms and tables.

  • list-view

    Toggle any Filament table between the default table and an alphabetical list view.
